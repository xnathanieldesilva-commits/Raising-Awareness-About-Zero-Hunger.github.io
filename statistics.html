Deaths and Hunger

Hunger is a serious problem that affects millions of people around the world. According to global hunger estimates, approximately 673 million people experienced hunger in 2024. Many families struggle to afford enough nutritious food, leaving them vulnerable to malnutrition and poor health. Children are especially at risk because their bodies and brains need proper nutrition to grow and develop. Undernutrition is linked to nearly half of all deaths among children under five years old, making it a major global health concern.

Hunger is often caused by several factors, including poverty, armed conflicts, climate change, natural disasters, and inequality. These problems can make food difficult to produce, transport, or afford. Millions of people continue to face food insecurity, meaning they do not always have reliable access to enough safe and nutritious food. Without action, many families will continue to suffer from hunger and its devastating effects. By raising awareness, supporting food assistance programmes, and addressing the root causes of hunger, we can help save lives and work towards a world where everyone has enough to eat.
